THE SIGNAL CHAIN

Four tools,
one workflow.

Every deliverable moves through the same chain — capture, retouch, cut, grade, mix — so stills and video come out sounding and looking like they belong together.

Photoshop

Frequency separation retouching, composite work, and print-ready color correction on every selected frame.

→ finishes the deliverable
